The BoltCutter is designed as an open top "glass cannon" battle bot. Its lack of armor is compensated by wild driving speeds and wild spinner speeds along with easy repair and rewiring due to the open design.
The model uses the brushless motor as the main weapon which host a mounting bracket for compatible spinner models. This can be hand measured or copied from the spinner model.
Things of importance:
-Intended orientation for the hull is the nub pointing to the ground (it acts as a front wheel, can be removed for spinner depression.
Make sure the ESP does not suffer from dragging on the ground).
-The spinner may hit the 5V power pin so make sure the ESP sags low enough or the spinner spins high enough to clear the first few pins. Or bend the wire.
-The brushed motors for driving need drill holes for the wires. Cut a common wire and pull it through the model. 
They also need something holding them up in the model, glue, printed holders or the wires can keep them up.
-A lot of wirings and drivers have been gluegunned or ziptied on top. You can make your own model more organized, but remember!
Battlbots need repair so encasing important wirings might not be the best idea in the actual tournament.
